THE PROBLEM

Contracts in the GCC are still managed over email and WhatsApp

The gap between how legal work should be done and how it actually happens has a real cost.

Weeks
from first request to signed contract — chased over email, WhatsApp, and shared folders
Zero
visibility into where a contract is stuck, who has it, and why it isn't moving
Silent
auto-renewals when notice deadlines pass unnoticed — locking businesses into unfavourable terms
🔄
Every contract reinvents the same process
Legal is the bottleneck even for routine NDAs and services agreements. Business stakeholders wait days for templates that should take hours.
📁
Signed contracts disappear into folders
Obligations, renewal dates, and contract values live in nobody's head after signing day. Missed deadlines are discovered too late.
⚖️
Bilingual is handled by hand — and it drifts
Arabic and English versions are managed separately. They diverge. When Arabic governs in a KSA court, that divergence becomes a liability.
✍️
Signing authority is assumed, not verified
Documents are executed by individuals not empowered to bind the company. The issue surfaces months later, usually in a dispute.

The market: GCC commercial legal services exceed $4B annually. No AI-native platform exists for the bilingual, Arabic-prevailing, KSA-jurisdictioned market. The largest players are English-only and built for Western legal systems.
